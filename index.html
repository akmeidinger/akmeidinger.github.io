About

Welcome to Glitches' Laboratory, where continuous experiments and trials are done with different art mediums to discover what works and what doesn’t. Here you can explore some of my past experiments along with tips and tricks for starting a new medium in art. Each page goes over a bit about the material, the forms it comes in, and sometimes the brands I’ve used, along with helpful learning advice. Best of luck with your experiments, new scientists! If you have any questions or want to share your own creations, feel free to fill out the contact form and reach out!
